Hello I am Teddy, typically known on the internet as Dragonblade316. I am a Programmer/Engineer in training who loves to dabble with anything I can get my hands on.

My main area of interest is robotics, where I have a couple of active projects. Over time, I hope to build some tools to make it easier for the average developer to pick up robotics. My long-term goal is product development for some sort of robotics or manufacturing machine company (think warehouse robots or 3d printers).

Since this is a little short, here are some fun facts about me:
- While I have loved computers and robots for most of my life, my first real exposure to programming and engineering was with the FRC team 5461 VERN where I eventually was able to attend the championship in Houston. I now volunteer as a CSA at the Idaho regional when I can.

- I am an avid Linux user and have found myself to be a fan of NixOS. Ironically, while I love the NixOS philosophy of making the system reproducible, I find that in many situations the Nix way of doing things is too much of a hassle, resulting in me occasionally finding ways to bypass Nix where it makes sense.

- I am a lover of the Rust programming language, but mainly because it seems to be the only programming language I have come across that “just works”. C++, Python, and sometimes Java all seem to have annoying ecosystems and/or type systems that make them considerably more annoying to work with, in my opinion.
